# Contractor and 1099 Tax Basics Reference

In the United States, an independent contractor is paid without tax withheld, reports that income themselves, and owes both income tax and self-employment tax on the profit. Clients who pay a contractor 600 or more in a year generally report it on a Form 1099. Because no tax is withheld along the way, contractors are expected to pay estimated tax through the year and to set money aside for it. This reference explains those basics in plain language.

This is general education about the United States system, not tax or legal advice, and rules change and vary by state. Numbers and thresholds shift year to year. Talk to a qualified tax professional about your own situation, and confirm current figures with the relevant authority.
